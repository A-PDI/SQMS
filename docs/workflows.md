# Business processes

This guide describes how the main processes run in SQMS and which controls the system enforces. Screens are
named as they appear in the navigation.

## 1. Sourcing a product (Sourcing projects > New sourcing project)

The nine-step wizard captures the request and produces the project, its documents and its checklist in one
transaction:

1. **Product** - existing internal part or a new one (part number, description, criticality).
2. **Supplier & site** - existing supplier and manufacturing site, or a new prospective supplier and site.
3. **Sourcing pathway** - A (company-controlled design), B (supplier standard product) or C (supplier product with
   company modifications).
4. **Reverse engineering** (Pathway A only) - when only a physical sample exists; identifies the sample.
5. **Risk assessment** - criticality and supplier status are taken from the records; the user scores complexity,
   technology novelty, supply chain, single source, regulatory impact and field history. The versioned model
   (`SPRA-1`) computes the level; safety-critical products are never below HIGH.
6. **Technical baseline** - drawing data (A) or supplier specification and guaranteed characteristics (B/C), special
   processes, and for Pathway C the delta requirements (baseline value, required value, acceptance criteria).
7. **Document requirements** - preview of the checklist generated by the active applicability policy, with the
   reason for every item.
8. **Team & due dates** - owner and members by role; due dates flow into the checklist tasks.
9. **Review & submit** - creates the project, the Product Sourcing Request (INT-01) and the Sourcing Path & Risk
   Assessment (INT-02) as native documents, and optionally submits them for intake review.

**Intake review** runs the `sourcing-project-review` workflow (Engineering and Supplier Quality in parallel). On
approval the project moves to In progress and the intake documents are approved.

**Checklist.** Each requirement is REQUIRED or CONDITIONAL with its reason, assignee and due date. Users create the
document from the requirement (native form or upload) or link an existing document. Waiving needs a written
justification and `project:waive_conditional` (conditional) or `project:waive_mandatory` (mandatory); waivers are
audited and listed in the approval package. Re-assessing risk records a new assessment (previous ones stay in
history) and re-applies the policy: new requirements are added, levels updated and items that no longer apply are
marked not applicable.

## 2. Document control (Document records, Document library)

Each document type defines ownership, applicable pathways, approvers, review workflow, supplier visibility,
retention policy, controlled template revisions and (optionally) a native form schema.

```
DRAFT --submit--> IN_REVIEW --approve--> APPROVED --release--> RELEASED --new revision--> (next revision DRAFT)
                      |  \--reject--> REJECTED --reopen--> DRAFT          RELEASED --supersede/obsolete--> SUPERSEDED / OBSOLETE
                      \--withdraw--> DRAFT
```

* Submission validates the native form against its schema version, requires a primary file for uploads, refuses
  files that are not scanned clean, and stores a SHA-256 hash of the frozen content.
* Approvers see exactly the submitted revision; decisions record the content reference.
* A released revision is never edited; a new revision is opened and the previous release stays in force until the
  new one is released.

## 3. Engineering definition (Products > supplier product > configuration revision)

* The baseline (drawing/specification, material, coating, heat treatment, performance) must be accepted by
  Engineering.
* Delta requirements must be acknowledged by the supplier (recorded with the representative's name; supplier users
  acknowledge directly in Phase 2) and verified by the company.
* ENG-04 (supplier baseline product specification), ENG-06 (delta specification) and ENG-08 (critical
  characteristics matrix) can be generated from the revision as native documents.

## 4. Product qualification and approval (Qualifications)

Opened from the project for the target configuration revision and site. Evidence: tests (planned with acceptance
criteria, result, review), first article inspections (counts, outcome, FAIR document, review), inspection results
per characteristic. Test results and FAIs cannot be reviewed by the person who recorded them.

The **approval gate** must pass before submission: baseline accepted; delta requirements acknowledged and verified;
configuration and site identified; supplier status allows approval; required documentation accepted or waived;
required tests and FAI accepted; no open blocking NCR or deviation. Submission runs the workflow chosen by
`qualification.workflowMatrix` (standard or high risk with management approval). Approval creates the
`ProductApproval`, the approval package with content hashes, a system-generated approval record (VAL-11), and sets
the supplier product to Approved.

## 5. Supplier change control (Change requests)

```
DRAFT -> SUBMITTED -> UNDER_REVIEW -> VALIDATION_REQUIRED -> (approval) -> APPROVED -> IMPLEMENTATION_VERIFIED -> CLOSED
            ^   \-> INFO_REQUIRED (request information) -/            \-> REJECTED -> CLOSED
            \-- WITHDRAWN (any open state)
```

* A change request applies to an approved source and copies the authorized revision into a **proposed** revision
  (status PROPOSED). Only one open change per authorized revision is allowed; the authorized revision cannot be
  edited.
* Impact assessments by discipline flag needs such as FAI, functional test, certificates, control plan or
  specification updates. The **requalification decision** sets the risk, generates the required documents and
  opens a requalification for the proposed revision.
* Submission for approval uses the same gate as product approval. Approval supersedes the previous revision and its
  product approval (both kept as history) and authorizes the proposed one; conditions, when given, become approval
  restrictions and the source becomes Conditionally approved.
* **Shipments of changed product are authorized only after implementation verification.**

## 6. Nonconformance, deviations and corrective action (Quality)

* **NCR**: OPEN -> CONTAINED -> UNDER_INVESTIGATION -> DISPOSITIONED -> CLOSED (or CANCELLED). Containment is
  recorded with completion; disposition is rework, return, scrap, repair or use-as-is. Repair and use-as-is require
  an approved **concession**. Internal notes are never returned to supplier users. Open NCRs flagged as blocking
  stop product approval.
* **Deviation / concession**: DRAFT -> SUBMITTED -> APPROVED / REJECTED, then EXPIRED (automatic at the end date)
  or CLOSED. Temporary deviations must be limited by an end date or a quantity.
* **SCAR (8D)**: DRAFT -> ISSUED -> RESPONSE_SUBMITTED -> ACCEPTED -> VERIFICATION -> CLOSED, with
  RESPONSE_REJECTED returning to the supplier; a NOT_EFFECTIVE verification also returns it there and raises the
  escalation level. The 8D sections D1-D7 are required to
  submit; the reviewer cannot be the person who entered the response; closure requires verified effectiveness.
  Overdue responses escalate on the schedule in `scar.escalationDays`.

## 7. Approvals, tasks and notifications

* **Approvals** lists steps assigned to the user or their roles, with due dates and escalation level. Decisions are
  Approve, Reject (reason required) or Request information; reassignment is recorded with a reason.
* **Tasks** collects checklist items, approvals and manual follow-ups; reminders are sent before due dates.
* Notifications are delivered in the application and by email according to each user's preferences.

## 8. Phase 2 supplier portal

With `SUPPLIER_PORTAL_ENABLED=true` (and an MFA provider in production) supplier users sign in to see their open
tasks, shared released documents, NCRs and SCARs made visible to them, submit 8D responses, acknowledge
requirements and documents, and raise change requests and deviation requests - always limited to their own
supplier by the server.
